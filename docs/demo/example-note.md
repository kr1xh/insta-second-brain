# example_creator - Classify bird calls from phone recordings

> [!summary]
> A weekend project: record bird calls with a phone, turn each clip into a spectrogram and train a small image classifier on it.

## What's shown
- Title card: "Your first audio ML project"
- Screen recording of a spectrogram notebook
- Accuracy plot ending at about 90% on 5 species

## What's said
1. Record 30 clips per species.
2. Convert each clip to a mel spectrogram.
3. Fine-tune a small pretrained image model.
4. Test on clips recorded on a different day.

The full notebook is gated ("comment BIRDS").
